Release date FAQ

Short answers to the questions that sit under a release calendar. Each game also has its own date page.

Questions

What do confirmed, scheduled, window and TBA mean?

Confirmed means a publisher page or a cited public list names the day. Scheduled means public release calendars agree and the day can still move. Window means a year or season has been stated without a day. TBA means no public date.

Does this list every game coming out?

No. Streetdate tracks notable releases people search for. It is not a complete Steam or eShop dump.

Which time zone are the dates in?

Dates are calendar dates from the publisher or public list, not a worldwide unlock hour. Streetdate does not invent a midnight time.

Is Streetdate an official publisher site?

No. Streetdate is independent of Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo and the studios named here.

Do you list wagers?

No. There are no sportsbook links or wagering offers on these pages.

Why are there Amazon links?

Some pages link to an Amazon.de search for a game or console using affiliate tag cynews24-21. A purchase can earn Streetdate a commission. Amazon sets the price.

When was the calendar last updated?

25 November 2026. Change the dates in games/data/releases.json and rebuild the pages when a publisher moves a game.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Grand Theft Auto VI launched on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not announced a PC date, so the PC version stays TBA.
