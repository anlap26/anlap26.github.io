Fable release date

Playground Games' new Fable is a fresh start in Albion, not a continuation of the Lionhead trilogy. The studio's FAQ dates the launch 23 February 2027 on Xbox Series X|S, Steam and PlayStation 5, with day-one access on Game Pass Ultimate. Premium and Collector's editions can start up to five days early, on 18 February 2027.
