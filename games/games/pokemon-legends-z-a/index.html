Pokémon Legends: Z-A release date

Pokémon Legends: Z-A launched on 16 October 2025 for Nintendo Switch and Switch 2. The Pokémon Company's press release that day says trainers and Pokémon move in real time, with moves used on command. A May 2025 news post had already named 16 October as the date.
