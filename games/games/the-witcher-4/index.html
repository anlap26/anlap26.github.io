The Witcher 4 release date

CD Projekt Red's next main Witcher game does not have a release day. In November 2026 the studio's joint CEO described 2028 as the target window and asked players to wait. Public comments put Ciri in the lead role, with Geralt no longer the centre of the story. Treat 2028 as a window, not a date.
