Has Grand Theft Auto VI been delayed?

The first trailer, published by Rockstar in December 2023, pointed to a 2025 window. Rockstar later dated the game 26 May 2026. On 6 November 2025 Rockstar moved the release to 19 November 2026, saying the extra months were for polish.
