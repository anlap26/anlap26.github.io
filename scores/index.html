Questions

What time zone are the written fixtures in?

The snapshot on each page is Europe/Nicosia. The live board converts kick-offs to the time zone of the phone or computer you are using.

Where do the scores come from?

Harbourline reads ESPN’s public scoreboard JSON in the browser and in a scheduled refresh of these pages. It is not affiliated with ESPN or the leagues.

Do you show betting odds?

No. Harbourline does not list odds, spreads or betting partners.
